# Meeting Notes Summarizer

Sample extension that shows the expected layout. Replace or delete it once real skills exist.

When the user shares meeting notes or a transcript:

1. List the decisions that were made, one line each.
2. List open questions that were not resolved.
3. List action items as a checklist with an owner and a due date. Write "unassigned" when no owner was named.
4. Keep the whole summary under one page.
